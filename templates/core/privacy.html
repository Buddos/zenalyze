Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Your mental health and personal reflections are profoundly private. We strictly do not sell, license, or market your personal wellness data to any third party.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to enter into the platform, including account credentials (username, email), mood ratings, journal reflections, exercise completions, and optional profile data.

2. How Your Information Is Protected

All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS encryption protocols. Private journal entries and personal mood notes are restricted to your authenticated user account.

3. Data Deletion Rights

You retain full ownership of your data. You may request the export or permanent deletion of your account and all associated wellness records at any time from your settings panel.
